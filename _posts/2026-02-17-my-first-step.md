## The Journey of a Data Architect

Hello, I am **Hooner - databaselee**. 

With over 15 years of experience as an **Database Solution Specialist**, I have spent my career designing robust data architectures and optimizing complex systems. However, the data landscape is evolving rapidly, and I am now expanding my horizons into the **Modern Data Stack**, specifically focusing on **Databricks**, **Python** and **Spark**.

### Why This Transition?

In today's ecosystem, the boundary between traditional RDBMS and Big Data is blurring. My goal is to bridge the gap between structured relational reliability and the scalability of Lakehouse architectures. 

I am documenting my journey of mastering:
* **PySpark & Delta Lake:** Moving beyond SQL to scalable data processing.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Transitioning on-premise expertise to Databricks environments.
* **Automation:** Leveraging Python to streamline ETL pipelines.
